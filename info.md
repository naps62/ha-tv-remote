## TV Remote Card

Sleek mobile Lovelace card for an NVIDIA Shield + webOS TV pairing.
Swipe-pad d-pad with shader-like visual feedback, app + TV-source launchers,
media transport, 5-step volume slider, webOS-aware Power highlight.

Config-from-UI is supported — click the "Edit" pencil on the card to open the
visual editor.

See the README for the full config schema.
